2. What Personal Data I Collect and Why

2.1 Server Log Data

When you visit this website, your web hosting provider automatically records technical data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This data is collected by the hosting provider for technical maintenance and security purposes. We do not have direct access to or control over this data.

2.2 Contact via Email

If you contact me using the email address listed on this website, any personal information you include in your message (such as your name, email address, or project details) is used to respond to your inquiry and, where applicable, to communicate with you about an artwork, commission, or other related matter.

2.3 Contact via WhatsApp

This website lists a WhatsApp number as an additional contact option. WhatsApp is a messaging service operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, 4 Grand Canal Square, Grand Canal Harbour, Dublin 2, Ireland ("Meta"). If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, please be aware of the following:

If you choose to contact me via WhatsApp, any information you provide, including your name, phone number, and message content, is processed in order to respond to your inquiry and, where applicable, to communicate with you about an artwork, commission, or other related matter.

Your communication is transmitted through and stored on WhatsApp's infrastructure. Meta processes personal data in accordance with its own privacy policy, which you can review at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

2.4 Newsletter Subscription via Brevo

This website offers an optional newsletter. If you choose to subscribe, your email address (and any other information you provide during sign-up) will be collected and processed by Brevo (formerly Sendinblue), an email marketing platform operated by Brevo SAS, 55 rue d’Amsterdam, 75008 Paris, France.

Brevo acts as a data processor on our behalf. Your data is used exclusively to send you our newsletter and is not used for any other purpose. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in every newsletter email.

For more information on how Brevo handles your data, please review Brevo’s privacy policy at: https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, the following legal bases apply to our data processing activities:

  • Server log data: Processing is based on my legitimate interest in maintaining a secure, stable, and functional website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Email contact: Processing is based on my legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry and managing correspondence (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where your inquiry concerns steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, processing may additionally be based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • WhatsApp contact: Processing is based on my legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry and managing correspondence (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where your inquiry concerns steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, processing may additionally be based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The processing carried out independently by Meta through WhatsApp is subject to Meta's own privacy policy and legal bases.

6. Data Retention

I retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Server log data is retained according to GitHub's applicable retention practices.
  • Emails are kept only as long as necessary to handle your inquiry and any related communication, unless longer retention is required by law.

Where you have specifically expressed an interest in receiving information about new artwork or future pieces, your email address may be retained for that purpose for as long as such communication remains relevant. You may request deletion of your contact details at any time, subject to any legal obligations requiring their retention.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, including the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding personal data I directly hold about you, where the respective legal requirements are met:

  • Right of access — request information about the personal data I hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — ask me to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to restriction — ask me to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — where applicable, request your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, please contact me at info@reginafroehlich.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.
